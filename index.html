CONTROLS + BETA NOTES

Start with Standard. Forgiving gives you more room to explore; Unkind raises the pressure. Difficulty, audio, resolution and reduced flashes are in Options.

Keyboard: WASD move · mouse aim / left click fire · 1–4 or wheel switch available equipment · R reload · Q or right click melee · Space / Shift dash · E use · M map · Tab peek · U mute · Esc pause.

Touch: left stick moves; drag on the right to aim. Hold FIRE to shoot and keep dragging to track enemies. DASH, MELEE, RELOAD and GUN are beside your thumb. USE appears for nearby interactions. MAP pauses while you get your bearings. Landscape gives you the clearest view.

Look around. Read the signs, follow the current objective, and use E or USE when an interaction appears. The map lets you choose between available destinations.

Keep this page open. Checkpoints work during the current session. Closing or reloading the page starts a new run.

For feedback: what felt great, where you got lost, whether a fight felt unfair, and any glitches or slowdowns. The pause and end screens can prepare version and session details to copy into your message.

Headphones recommended. Contains flashing effects; reduce them in Options.

WHAT TO CHECK IN THIS VERSION

0.1.2 — Navigation HUD. The guide has its own compact space, consistent icons and readable instructions. This phase changes presentation; enemy audio remains pending its separate redesign.

  1. At a junction, glance up: can you read both the destination and the direction immediately?
  2. Turn fully around, then approach your destination. Are the turn-around and nearby symbols clear?
  3. In Fever Theatre, open M and choose a different system. Check the selected name and long labels.
  4. Compare dark corridors, neon rooms and the exterior. Is the panel readable without drawing too much attention?
  5. Compare Heart — aftermath and Garden gates — after the guardian. Boss information must stay at the edge; guidance must not overlap it.
  6. On a phone, check portrait and landscape. Text should remain readable; HUD elements should stay apart.
