# ASHFALL // NEON WARD — Credits

## Audio and recordings

AUDIO CREDITS

[Dark Descent — Matthew Pablo](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-descent). [Horror Sound Library — Little Robot Sound Factory](https://opengameart.org/content/horror-sound-effects-library). [Shotgun / reload recordings — Mike Koenig (Soundbible), submitted by qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-shoot-reload). Licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Edited, layered, pitched, filtered and mixed for this game; score tempo, loop and mastering adjusted. The Heart finale adds three tempo-matched arrangements and a layered collapse sequence derived from these same recordings. The exterior adds original soft foliage air, shore wash, distant city, diesel, tyre skid and fire beds. Cerberus layers the existing voice recordings at distinct pitches and adds an original beam texture. Its dedicated score, Three Throats, is an original 162 BPM composition with distorted bass strings, metallic percussion, choir textures and a Phrygian bell motif; percussion and voice textures use transformed samples from the credited effects bank. It contains no samples of the ward or Heart music. The hell threshold adds original synthesized stereo storm wind and rolling thunder. Beta 0.1.1 adds original nonverbal creature phonation, turbulent breath and throat resonances, layered with short grains from the credited nonvocal flesh/bone recordings. The new creature bank contains no human vocal recordings.

Outdoor wildlife: [Field cricket — Thatcher](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Field_cricket_Gryllus_pennsylvanicus.ogg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); [Tawny owl — Vianney Bajart](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strix_aluco_male.oga), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Trimmed, filtered, faded, encoded and spatially mixed. The cricket and owl audio adaptations remain available under their respective licenses.

Additional CC0 recordings: [qubodup](https://opengameart.org/content/15-vocal-male-strainhurtpainjump-sounds), [Ogrebane](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-2), [rubberduck](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx).
